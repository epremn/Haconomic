Hackonomics

In this project, I have created an app that helps to target two topics. Education and Finance. In the Education Section, I have created a survey that will check if your spending the right amount of money either in public or private school. Another survey is the Finance survey section where it asks you to place the amount of money that you spent in specific categories and then take these amounts and take the total. It create a pie chart by take each part of the total and then mergining it one by one and print them out as a chart.
